# 🌍 Overview

Welcome to my geospatial portfolio.

This repository brings together my work in **Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing, Earth Observation, Climate Analytics, Environmental Modelling, and Spatial Data Science**. It showcases research, open-source projects, interactive dashboards, publications, and practical geospatial solutions developed to support environmental sustainability and evidence-based decision-making.

---

# 👨‍💼 About Me

I am **Nnachi Ikwuo Nnachi**, a **GIS Specialist**, **Remote Sensing Analyst**, and **Earth Observation Researcher** with over five years of professional experience applying geospatial technologies to solve environmental and climate-related challenges.

My research focuses on:

- 🌍 Climate Change
- 🌳 Forest Monitoring
- 🔥 Urban Heat Dynamics
- 🌫 Air Quality
- 🛰 Earth Observation
- 🌱 Environmental Sustainability
- 📊 Spatial Data Science
